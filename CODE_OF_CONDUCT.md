# Code of conduct

OpenEight welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code
applies to repository issues, pull requests, project discussions and other spaces
where someone represents this project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
Ask questions in good faith, explain decisions, and make room for people who are
learning. Respect privacy and people's requests to end unwanted contact.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, sustained
personal attacks, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are not
acceptable. Disagreement about a technical decision is welcome; intimidation and
abuse are not.

## Reporting concerns

Report harassment or other community concerns privately to
[hello@openeight.ai](mailto:hello@openeight.ai). Reports will be reviewed by
the project maintainer. Do not post sensitive conduct reports, personal details
or allegations in public issues.
For a suspected software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Handling reports

The project maintainer should acknowledge a report, gather only the information
needed to understand it, and protect the reporter's identity as far as practical.
Report information should be shared only with those needed to investigate or act;
absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

Actions should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Maintainers may
request a correction, issue a warning, remove abusive content, temporarily limit
participation, or exclude someone for serious or repeated misconduct. Explain the
reason and conditions to the affected person when safe to do so. Reports made in
good faith must not lead to retaliation. A person may ask for reconsideration
through the same private contact with additional context.
